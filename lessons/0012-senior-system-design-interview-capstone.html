Tình huống phỏng vấn

“Thiết kế API flash sale cho 100 concurrent request, stock 10. Không oversell. Không dùng payment thật. Kafka đôi lúc unavailable. Làm sao?” Đừng trả lời bằng danh sách Spring/Kafka/Kubernetes. Bắt đầu bằng invariant: accepted quantity không vượt Available Quantity.

Một câu trả lời senior có cấu trúc

  1. Scope: modular monolith, PostgreSQL source of truth, một Product mỗi Order; không microservice/payment trong MVP.
  2. HTTP contract: POST Order, idempotency key per Customer, 201 first accept, 200 replay, 409 business conflicts, Problem Details errors.
  3. Correctness: one transaction resolves key, snapshots Product, conditional-decrements Inventory, inserts Order + outbox. Conditional update is the anti-oversell mechanism.
  4. Messaging: transactional outbox; asynchronous publisher retries; consumer deduplicates event ID; claim at-least-once, not magical exactly-once.
  5. Read performance: bounded pagination, stable order, query-specific projection, indexes from actual filters/order, N+1 statement budget.
  6. Operations: health/readiness/metrics/trace ID, Compose smoke, Kind rollout and in-cluster concurrency smoke.

Trade-off drill

QuestionGood senior answer
Why not Redis lock?PostgreSQL is source of truth and atomic conditional update already protects one-row invariant; extra distributed lock adds failure modes.
Why not microservices?Current boundary/operational need does not justify distributed transactions/deployment overhead; outbox keeps future event boundary explicit.
Why not optimistic lock?Hot SKU turns conflict into retry storm; atomic SQL is simpler for this invariant.
What if scale grows?Measure hot keys, DB lock waits, p95, query plans; then consider queue/reservation/partitioning only with concrete requirements.

Mock interview practice

Trả lời trong 2–3 phút, không nhìn code:

  1. Walk me through Order creation and every side effect.
  2. Prove no oversell under two replicas and 100 requests.
  3. Explain duplicate request, Kafka down, and consumer duplicate cases.
  4. Which metric/log/test would you inspect after a report “order succeeded but audit missing”?
  5. What is intentionally out of scope and why?

Rubric tự chấm

Đạt khi câu trả lời nêu được invariant, implementation mechanism, failure mode, test evidence, và trade-off. Chưa đạt nếu chỉ nói “dùng transaction/lock/Kafka” mà không giải thích timing, source of truth, hoặc failure behavior.

Gửi câu trả lời cho câu 1 hoặc capstone 6 phần. Mình sẽ đóng vai interviewer, phản biện và tạo learning record khi bạn thể hiện được reasoning độc lập. Primary sources: Spring transactions và PostgreSQL isolation.
