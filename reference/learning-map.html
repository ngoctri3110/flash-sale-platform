Roadmap: Senior Backend → Tech Lead — module, evidence và mock interview theo từng phase.
  1. Transaction boundary & atomic decrement
  2. Idempotency, replay & rollback
  3. Concurrency strategies
  4. JPA N+1, fetch plans and DTO projections
  5. REST validation & Problem Details
  6. PostgreSQL, Flyway, indexes & pagination
  7. Transactional Outbox & Kafka delivery
  8. Clean Architecture & architecture tests
  9. Docker Compose, observability & tracing
  10. Kubernetes/Kind, probes, rollout & smoke
  11. Reviewing AI-generated backend code
  12. Senior system design & interview capstone

Do lessons in order. A learning record is created only after you answer the practice questions or demonstrate the skill in code.
